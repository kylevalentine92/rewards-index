What this dashboard shows

South Africans belong to around ten rewards programmes each, and most comparisons only tell you which one pays the most. This index measures something different: how much effort it takes to get the value a programme promises. Every programme gets a Complication Score out of 100. Higher means harder.

The score combines eight things: how complicated earning is, how hard tiers are to reach and keep, the habits you need to build, how hard it is to redeem, how easily value expires, how clearly you can see where you stand, what you must pay or hold to qualify, and how often the rules change.

How to read a score

Simple (0 to 30): swipe and save. Little to track.

Manageable (31 to 55): a few rules to keep an eye on.

Complex (56 to 75): real effort to stay maximised.

Maze (76 to 100): the value is there, but it takes ongoing work to unlock.

A high score is not a verdict on value. Some of the most complicated programmes also pay the most.

Who it is scored for

The same programme is easier for someone who already banks, shops or lives the way it rewards. Use Score for at the top to switch between:

Overall: the average across all four personas. Start here.

Entry, Mass market, Middle income, Affluent: income-based personas with different everyday habits. Only the habit burden part of the score changes.

Persona habits are early assumptions and will be replaced with real behaviour as people use the platform.

Good to know

Ratings come from public programme rules and South African press coverage researched in October 2026. Rules change often, so some details may already be out of date. Programmes marked low confidence had thinner sources.

This is an independent index. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the programmes it scores, and it is not financial advice.

    How the score works

    Each programme is rated 1 to 5 on eight dimensions. Each rating becomes (rating minus 1) divided by 4, times the weight, and the eight are added up. All 1s scores 0 and all 5s scores 100.

    Habit burden is the only dimension that changes by persona. For each behaviour a programme asks for, the gap between what it requires and what the persona already does is weighted by effort (passive, admin, taking a product, changing your lifestyle) and frequency (weekly, monthly, annual).

    The score measures effort, not value or fairness. A programme can be complex for good reasons. The Reward Gap is shown separately so readers can judge fairness for themselves.
